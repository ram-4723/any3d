# Credits — Porsche 911 GT3 R (992) sound kit

Every clip in this kit is a **real recording**, used under a free licence
(CC0, public domain, CC BY 3.0, or CC BY 4.0). Nothing here is synthesised
or given a made-up credit. Clips were trimmed, faded and loudness-normalised
(target about −16 LUFS, peaks below −1 dBTP) for the site; that is an
adaptation, so CC BY works still need the attribution below.

Freesound has some even closer recordings, but original-file download
required a login, so those were **not** used. Wikimedia Commons, OpenGameArt
and Kenney originals were used instead. CC BY-SA race/paddock beds were
available and were skipped in favour of CC0 / public domain / CC BY.

## Honest gaps

- No freely downloadable recording of a 992 GT3 R 4.2-litre race engine under CC0 / CC BY was found. The engine and ambient beds are a road-going Porsche 911 GT3 RS (991) flat-six (Automotion Media, CC BY 3.0). A 992 GT3 with a Fi Exhaust aftermarket system was available but was not used, because the exhaust is modified.
- The engine slice is a mid-session rev / crackle, not a cold start.
- There is no pit-lane or Daytona atmosphere recording under a preferred licence (several race-car beds on Commons are CC BY-SA and were skipped). debut and ambient are cut from the same GT3 RS exhaust recording.
- The cockpit clip is a workshop ratchet, not a racing sequential gearbox.
- The brake clip is a stone scrape, not a recorded race disc or carbon brake.
- The radiator clip is an industrial ventilation fan, not a car radiator fan.
- The fuel clip is water poured from bottles, not a quick-fill coupling.
