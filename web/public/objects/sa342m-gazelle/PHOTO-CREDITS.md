# Real Wikimedia Commons photos — sa342m-gazelle

French SA342M photographs, plus a clearly identified SA342M1/Viviane variant for sight/weapon details. Engine photo 2 is an exposed Turbomeca Astazou family reference: exact XIV M subvariant is NOT established by the Commons metadata. The gearbox is enclosed in the available airframe views and is intentionally unmapped.

## Reuse
Each image retains the license specified below. Preserve author attribution and follow ShareAlike where applicable. Images are real photographs, not renders or AI-generated images. Derivatives were cropped only as disclosed, resized without upscaling to maximum 2000px long side, and JPEG-recompressed at quality 85. Several label crops may share an original source; no claim is made that every file is a different camera exposure.
