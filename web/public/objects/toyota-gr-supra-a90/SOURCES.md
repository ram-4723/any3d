## Conflicting / market-dependent figures (please read)
- **Power:** launch 3.0 (2019–2020 MY) = 335 hp US / 340 PS (250 kW) Europe & Japan. From 2021 MY (US) / 2020 update (Japan) = 382 hp / 387 PS (285 kW). Wikipedia notes that some markets kept the 340 PS version. Final Edition (Japan/Europe) = 441 PS (324 kW). It was first announced as 435 PS (320 kW), and the US "MkV Final Edition" stays at 382 hp.
- **Torque:** 500 Nm (Japan/Europe, all years); US 365 lb-ft (2020) → 368 lb-ft (2021+). Toyota Canada and the 45th-Anniversary release say 367 lb-ft. The 2021 US release says 368.
- **Acceleration:** Toyota quotes 0–100 km/h in 4.3 s (EU/Japan, 340 PS auto). US quotes 0–60 mph in 4.1 s (2020) and 3.9 s (2021+ auto) / 4.2 s (manual). Car and Driver measured 3.7 s (2020, 1-ft rollout). The EU Final Edition is 4.3 s, while a 3.0 MT on the Italian spec sheet is 4.6 s.
- **Top speed:** 250 km/h limited (Europe) vs 155 mph ≈ 249 km/h limited (US). The Final Edition is quoted at 275 km/h on the Toyota DE/IT spec pages (seen in search excerpts only).
- **Kerb weight:** Japan RZ 1,520 kg (2019) and 1,520–1,530 kg (2025). EU 1,495 kg without driver / 1,570 kg ECE. US 3,397 lb (≈1,541 kg, 2020) / 3,400 lb (2021). The site uses the range 1,495–1,530 kg.
- **Dimensions:** Japan 4,380 x 1,865 x 1,295 mm. EU 2019 press kit 4,379 x 1,854 x 1,292 mm. Current Toyota DE page width 1,867 mm. The site uses Japan figures.
- **Auction buyer:** Wikipedia says Craig Jackson (Barrett-Jackson CEO) bought the car. Toyota/Carscoops/Motoring Research say **John Staluppi** won it and took delivery. We use Staluppi. Craig Jackson was the auctioneer, quoted in the release.
- **VIN:** the brief mentioned "20190001". Toyota and Barrett-Jackson say the VIN **ends in 20201** ("2020" model year + car "1").
- **India price:** The GR Supra was **never officially launched in India**, so there is no ex-showroom price. ₹85–95 lakh is Autocar India's Feb 2020 estimate (CarDekho lists ₹85 lakh "expected"). It must not be shown as a launch price.

## Deliberately excluded (not verifiable / not about the A90)
- **Fast & Furious / Paul Walker:** linked to the orange **Mk4 (A80)** Supra, not the A90. Toyota only says the 45th Anniversary Edition pays tribute to "the MkIV model of big screen fame", so the fun fact is worded that way and there is no celebrity link.
- **Indian celebrity owners:** no reliable source found naming an Indian celebrity who owns an A90. One report (Yohan Poonawalla) concerns a **Mk4** Supra, so it was excluded.
- **"Only N exist"** style claims beyond Toyota-stated edition sizes were not used.
