# Credits — Toyota GR Supra 3.0 sound kit

Every clip in this kit is a **real recording**, used under a free licence
(CC0, public domain, CC BY 3.0, or CC BY 4.0). Nothing here is synthesised
or given a made-up credit. Clips were trimmed, faded and loudness-normalised
(target about −16 LUFS, peaks below −1 dBTP) for the site; that is an
adaptation, so CC BY works still need the attribution below.

Freesound has some even closer recordings, but original-file download
required a login, so those were **not** used. Wikimedia Commons, OpenGameArt
and Kenney originals were used instead. CC BY-SA race/paddock beds were
available and were skipped in favour of CC0 / public domain / CC BY.

## Honest gaps

- No freely downloadable B58 or A90 GR Supra recording under CC0 / CC BY was found. The engine, exhaust and ambient beds are a Toyota Supra (A80) 2JZ (Fabio Rojas Pichardo, CC BY 3.0). A BMW 335i turbo I6 (closer B58-family architecture) was available; the 2JZ was used instead for the closer Supra character, which the brief allows.
- The turbo whoosh is a Peugeot 406 with an HKS blow-off valve, not a B58 twin-scroll or a Supra BOV.
- Related internal labels share files (one 2JZ start across block/head/pistons/crank/caps/cover; one exhaust run across exhaust/exhman/cat; one mechanical click across the accessory-drive group; one spring across the chassis/suspension group). Captions say so.
- The gearbox clip is a workshop ratchet. The car uses a ZF 8HP automatic — there is no sequential-shift recording here.
- Accessory-drive labels (belt, pulley, tens, alt, ac) use a short tool/mechanical sound, not a recorded belt or rotating accessory.
- The fan / radiator / coolant-pump clips are an industrial ventilation fan.
- The fuel-tank clip is water poured from bottles.
- This object has no tyre and no door labels, so no tyre-screech or door-close was added (those were mentioned in the brief, but inventing labels would be wrong).
- The shared SoundManager plays the cinematic reveal from a label id engine. This object has no engine label (the block is block), and the shared code was not changed, so the intro stays silent even when sound is on unless you later extend the manager.
