## Uncertainties
Construction dates and Nau Lakha currency accounts differ. Labels do not invent a reconciliation. Orange highlights and flags are artistic additions rather than a documented paint survey. Exterior anchor positions are geometric approximations; unseen interiors/adjacent buildings are not claimed to be reconstructed.
