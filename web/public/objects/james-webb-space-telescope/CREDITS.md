The mesh was not rebuilt. Studio lighting and a charcoal cyclorama were added; imported Maya-style materials (including gold `mirror_blinn` and sunshield `shld_silv1` / `shld_pink1`) were converted to Principled BSDF with physically based metallic and roughness values, and imported emission strengths were zeroed so parts do not glow. 144 turntable frames are rendered in Blender Cycles at 1600×900, 256 samples with adaptive sampling (threshold 0.008, minimum 48) and OpenImageDenoise, from an elevated three-quarter camera about 22 degrees downward.

This is an illustrative 3D model for a frame-based viewer, based on NASA's official public 3D resource. It is not a flight-engineering CAD model.
